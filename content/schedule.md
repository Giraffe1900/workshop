# Dates to Remember!

These dates are fixed, but the order in which you do the course topics and submit your other assignments is up to you.

## Getting Started

- **Sept 8th:** You can access the pre-recorded introduction and syllabus tour.
- **Sept 9th, 5PM:** Info session with the instructor, where you can ask ANY questions and she’ll walk you through the assignments. *(Note: it is helpful to have viewed the syllabus tour first!)* Come with questions, ask anything.

## Your Choice of IPs

Between **Sept 8th and Dec 15th**, you need to submit **2 additional IPs of YOUR choice**. You schedule your own work and decide your own submission dates. This is intended to make your busy adult lives easier by handing over control of the workflow and scheduling to you—but please, **DO NOT leave things until the last minute.**

## Final Presentations

On the **week of December 1st** (time/day TBA) we have our concluding synchronous meeting to present the group (or individual) final “edtechdev” digital tool development project. Each group has **10 minutes** to present their work.

For those who cannot attend in real-time, their teammates will present the group project and share constructive feedback on others’ projects. If you absolutely cannot attend that final class meeting, please make sure you notify me in advance of that last class.
